AboutHello

I am an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student in the Department of Biological Sciences at KAIST, working in the Yoon Lab. My research sits at the intersection of RNA biology and neural development, with a focus on N6-methyladenosine (m6A) RNA methylation. I combine wet-lab experiments with computational analysis of high-throughput sequencing data.
